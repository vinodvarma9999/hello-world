          Bank SMS

          Tap 📋 Paste bank SMS on the dashboard after copying a bank message. Spendly reads the amount, payee and date on your phone; the SMS itself isn't saved or sent anywhere.

          iPhone: copy bank SMS automatically
          1. Open the Shortcuts app → Automation → + → Message.
          2. Tap Message Contains, type debited, choose Run Immediately, then Next → New Blank Automation.
          3. Add the action Copy to Clipboard and set its input to Shortcut Input (pick Content if asked).
          4. Add Show Notification with the text “Bank SMS copied – open Spendly”. Tap Done.
          5. Repeat for other words your bank uses, e.g. spent or Sent Rs.

          Then open Spendly and tap Paste bank SMS. iPhone asks you to allow the paste each time.
